# Testing checklist

For running by hand in a browser once the site is live at
https://classic.job-dox.ai. No developer tools needed, and nothing here needs
to be typed into a terminal. Work down the list in order and tick as you go.

If a step does not do what it says it should, write down the step number and
what happened instead. That is more useful than a guess at the cause.

## Before you start

Use a normal browser window rather than a private one, on a desktop or laptop.
There is a phone section near the end that you will do second. If you have
recently visited the site, the browser may be holding an old copy of it: hold
Shift and click reload once at the start, which forces a fresh copy.

## Part 1: the page loads

1. Go to https://classic.job-dox.ai
   The page should appear on a near black background with the words "JobDox
   University" at the top left and a small red "CLASSIC" tag beside them.

2. Look at the top of the main column.
   It should read "JobDox Classic" in small red capitals, then "Training
   Guides" as a large heading, then a sentence of introduction.

3. Count the headings down the main column.
   There should be eight, in this order: Projects, Budget and Billing, Users
   and Access, Settings and Configuration, Reporting and Search, Field
   Operations and Time Tracking, Drying, Video Tutorials.

4. Scroll to the very bottom.
   Every section should have guide cards under it, and the page should end with
   a copyright line. Nothing should be blank, and no heading should sit above
   an empty space.

5. Check the total number of cards.
   There are 41. You do not have to count them one by one here; Part 6 walks
   through all 41 individually.

6. Look at the browser tab.
   The title should read "JobDox Classic Training Guides". The tab will have no
   small icon next to it. That is expected and is not a fault: no Classic icon
   has been drawn yet, and the only icons available belong to the other product,
   which must not appear here.

## Part 2: the cards open

7. Click any card, for example "How to Create a New Job" under Projects.
   The guide should open in a NEW tab. The guides page should still be sitting
   there in the original tab, at the same scroll position, unchanged.

8. Close the new tab and return to the guides page.
   Nothing should have moved or reset.

9. Right click a card and choose "Open link in new tab".
   It should offer that option and it should work. If the browser offers no
   link options at all on a card, that is a fault worth reporting.

## Part 3: the sidebar

10. Look at the list of eight categories on the left.
    One of them should be highlighted in red with a soft red background. On
    first arrival it should be "Projects", the first one.

11. Click "Drying" in the sidebar.
    The page should slide down to the Drying section and the Drying category
    should become the highlighted one.

12. Click each of the other seven categories in turn.
    Each should jump to its own section, and each should end up highlighted.
    The heading you land on should sit a little below the top edge of the
    screen rather than jammed against it.

13. Now scroll slowly down the page with the mouse wheel or trackpad, without
    clicking anything.
    The highlight in the sidebar should move down the list on its own as each
    new section comes up. Exactly one category should be highlighted at any
    moment, and it should never go blank.

14. Scroll all the way to the bottom and stop.
    A category should still be highlighted. An empty sidebar at the bottom of
    the page is a fault.

## Part 4: search

15. Click the box at the top of the sidebar that reads "Search guides" and
    type: drying
    The page should narrow to just two cards, both under a Drying heading.
    Every other section should vanish completely, heading included.

16. Look at the sidebar while that search is still in the box.
    Only "Drying" should remain in the category list. The other seven should
    be gone, because there is nothing left for them to jump to.

17. Clear the box completely.
    All 41 cards, all eight headings and all eight categories should come back.

18. Type: mobile
    You should get four cards: two under Projects and two under Video
    Tutorials. The two under Projects each carry a small red "MOBILE" tag.
    This one is worth doing because those tags are part of what is searched.

19. Clear the box, then type: zzzz
    Every card should disappear and a line of grey text should appear reading
    "No guides match that search." All eight categories should disappear from
    the sidebar too.

20. Clear the box one more time.
    Everything should return. Nothing should be left hidden.

21. Type a capitalised word, for example: DRYING
    It should find the same two cards as the lowercase version. Search should
    not care about capital letters.

## Part 5: keyboard only

This part matters. Do not touch the mouse at all until step 27.

22. Click once on an empty part of the page, then press the Tab key repeatedly
    and watch where the focus goes.
    Something should always be visibly outlined in red as you tab. If you ever
    cannot see what is focused, that is a fault.

23. Keep pressing Tab until you reach the search box.
    It should be reachable, and it should show a red outline when it is.

24. Type into it from the keyboard.
    The cards should narrow exactly as they did with the mouse.

25. Clear it, then keep pressing Tab past the sidebar and into the cards.
    Every card should be reachable by Tab, one after another, each showing a
    red outline. You should be able to walk from the first card to the last
    without skipping any and without getting stuck.

26. Stop on any card and press Enter.
    That guide should open in a new tab, exactly as clicking it would.

27. Press Shift and Tab together a few times.
    Focus should walk backwards through the same items in reverse.

## Part 6: phone width

Do this on a real phone if you can. If not, make the browser window on your
computer as narrow as it will go, roughly the width of a phone.

28. Look at where the categories are.
    The sidebar should no longer be a column down the left. It should have
    become a row of small rounded pills sitting above the guides, wrapping onto
    more than one line, with the search box above them.

29. Look at the cards.
    At the narrowest widths they should be one per row, full width. At a middle
    width, roughly a small tablet, they should be two per row. On a full
    computer screen, three per row. There should be no width at which a card is
    squashed so narrow that its title is unreadable.

30. Tap a category pill.
    It should jump to that section just as the sidebar did, and it should
    highlight.

31. Type in the search box at phone width.
    Filtering should work the same, and pills for empty sections should
    disappear.

32. Check that nothing runs off the side of the screen.
    You should never have to scroll sideways. If you can push the page left and
    right, that is a fault.

33. Turn the phone from upright to sideways and back.
    The text should not suddenly jump to a different size.

## Part 7: every link, one at a time

THIS IS THE PART THAT CANNOT BE CHECKED ANY OTHER WAY. A guide that has been
deleted, moved, or had its sharing turned off looks completely normal on this
page: the card is there, it is clickable, and nothing about the code can tell
that the other end is gone. The only way to know is to open all 41.

Click each one, confirm something real loads at the other end, then close the
tab and tick the box. "Something real" means the guide or video actually plays
or displays. A sign in wall, a "you need access" message, a "page not found",
or an empty player all count as failures. Write down which ones fail.

THE FIVE GOOGLE DRIVE VIDEOS ARE THE MOST FRAGILE LINKS ON THIS PAGE and are
marked below. They are files sitting in one person's Google Drive, shared by
link. They stop working if that sharing setting is changed, if the file is
moved or renamed, or if that person leaves and their account is closed. Unlike
the Scribe guides and the YouTube videos, they are not on a platform built for
publishing. If any of the five fails, the fix is not on this website: somebody
has to go and find the file. Consider moving those five to a company owned
location before this becomes urgent.

## When you are done

Write down, in one message:

- Any step number that did not behave as described, and what happened instead.
- The number of any of the 41 links that failed to open something real.
- Anything that looked wrong to you even if no step above mentions it. A fresh
  pair of eyes catches things a checklist does not.

If every step behaved and all 41 links opened, say so plainly. That is a pass
and the site is ready to hand to customers.
